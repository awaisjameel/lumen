# Interface redesign, 19 October 2026

The interface was redesigned screen by screen on design boards first, approved
by the owner on 16 October 2026, and then rebuilt to match them. It was merged
as pull request #23 (issue #24). This record holds what was decided, where the
boards are, and where the app departs from them on purpose; the code and its
comments hold the rest.

## The boards

The approved design, kept outside the repository, has 21 boards: Foundations, Browse and view (Library, Viewer, Video, Search),
Collections (People, Person, Things, Places, Events, Albums and the shared
Collection header), Tools (Duplicates, Compression, Trash, Organise), Settings
and Blocked, privacy (PIN), first run, and the shared states. Each board shows
its states (empty, loading, partial failure, error, very large counts) at
widths from 720 to 1920 px in both themes. A screen that changes should be
checked against its board at 1440 and 800 px, in light and dark.

## Decisions

- **Themes.** "Daylight" and "Darkroom", with navy-tinted neutrals so the
  photos carry the colour, and one accent, cobalt unless the user chooses
  another. The warm gold of the brand mark (`--lumen-light`) is reserved for
  Lumen's own activity and AI suggestions, never for a control.
  `frontend/src/design/tokens.css` is the system; there is no second copy.
- **Shell.** The page is an inset sheet beside the sidebar. Below 1024 px the
  sidebar becomes a rail of icons and the Details panel a floating sheet;
  below the minimum window (720 × 480) navigation opens as a drawer.
- **Selection** shows in a floating dock (`shared/SelectionDock.vue`) only while
  something is selected, instead of a permanent bar. The Details panel slides
  in beside the content, and with several photos selected it steps through
  them to correct one at a time.
- **No Videos page.** The Library's All / Photos / Videos filter replaces it;
  `/videos` opens the Library on Videos, and the palette (Ctrl K) has
  "Show videos".
- **Library header.** The top bar holds the title with its live count, search
  and the Details toggle; the filter and zoom sit in a view bar under it.
- **Places** has an offline dot atlas (`places/atlasData.ts`, regenerated by
  `scripts/build_atlas.py` from the bundled GeoNames catalogue); no map tiles
  are fetched.
- **One shared part each:** `Chip`, `CoverMosaic`, `CollectionHero`,
  `Segmented` (also the underline tabs), `FilterField` and `ViewBar` replace
  their per-screen copies.

## What the engine gained

Date and place corrections that can be restored (migration 0039), undoable
unblocking, Trash grouped by the day items were trashed, recent searches as an
engine setting, blocked entries with their count and cover, search results
that name the people and description they matched, a place name for each day,
a person's companions (`Store.PersonFacts`) and a collection's top places
(`Store.TopPlaces`). Blocking applies to all of them: a blocked or covered
person, or a blocked place name, is never named. Each new read has a ceiling
in `internal/store/testdata/library-budgets.txt`.

## Kept deliberately against the boards

- **People** keeps the "Show hidden people" link: there must be a way back
  after hiding the last person.
- **Blocked and Organise** keep breadcrumb headers, the shared page header
  pattern.
- **Duplicates** keeps a floating "Show more groups" button, because its list
  is virtualised.
- **Person** keeps the view bar and search field that every other collection
  page has, and has no "Browse the full Library" footer.
- **Media tiles** keep literal scrim colours over photos, which must not
  change with the theme.

## Verification

`scripts/lint.sh` in full (945 frontend tests), `go test -race` for
`internal/core`, `internal/store` and `services`, and the end-to-end suites
against a production build: collections, interface, viewer, file-tools, tour,
onboarding, organise, customise and showcase. The tour visits every route at
four widths and fails on sideways overflow, a console error or any request
that leaves the PC.
